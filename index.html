Good to know

Questions, answered.

What kind of photo should I send?

Any clear, well-lit photo where faces are visible. Phone photos are fine. Unsure? Send a few and we'll pick the one that engraves best.

Can I add names, dates or a quote?

Yes, included in the price. Names, dates, a wish, a lyric, a doodle or a hashtag. Larger sizes leave more room for text.

Which size should I choose?

6″ for a single or couple portrait, 7″ for a portrait plus a quote, 8″ for three or more people. 5″ is a lovely desk piece.

How long does it take?

Each piece is made to order and dispatched within 24 hours of the artwork being finalised. We share tracking on WhatsApp.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, worldwide. Send your city and country and we'll confirm shipping before you pay.

How do I pay?

On WhatsApp, once the design and shipping are agreed. Only pay to details shared from our official WhatsApp or Instagram.
